## Your Certification Stack — What Each One Covers

**AWS AI Practitioner (AIF-C01) — October 2026**
Covers AI and ML fundamentals, generative AI concepts, foundation models, Amazon Bedrock and its ecosystem (Agents, Knowledge Bases, Guardrails), responsible AI principles,
and AWS security and governance for AI systems. Validates you understand the AI landscape and can work with AWS AI services at a conceptual and applied level.

---

**AWS FDE Validated Certification — When company announces**
Covers deploying and operationalising AI systems directly at enterprise clients. Three pathways — ground (foundations of AI deployment), orchestrate (agent workflows and multi-model systems),
prove (monitoring, red-teaming, and production hardening). Validates client-facing AI engineering capability, which is exactly what you do at Publicis Sapient.

---

**AWS Data Engineer Associate (DEA-C01) — December 2026**
Covers the full AWS data engineering stack — S3, Glue, Glue Data Catalog, Redshift, Athena, Kinesis, EMR, DynamoDB, Lambda, Step Functions, and Lake Formation. 
Validates you can design, build, and manage data pipelines and analytics solutions on AWS end to end.

---

**Databricks Certified Spark Developer Associate — March 2027**
Covers Apache Spark fundamentals using Python — DataFrame API, transformations and actions, Spark SQL, joins, aggregations, performance tuning, and the Spark execution model 
including DAGs, shuffles, and partitioning. Validates hands-on PySpark coding ability which is mandatory in many DE JDs.

---

**Databricks Data Engineer Associate — June 2027**
Covers the Databricks Lakehouse platform — Delta Lake (ACID transactions, time travel, schema evolution), structured streaming, Databricks workflows and job orchestration,
Unity Catalog for data governance, and building production pipelines on the Databricks platform. Validates end-to-end data engineering on the modern lakehouse architecture.

---

**SnowPro Core — September 2027**
Covers Snowflake architecture (virtual warehouses, cloud services layer, storage layer), data loading and unloading, Snowpipe, streams and tasks, data sharing,
performance optimisation, security and access control, and Snowflake-specific SQL features. Validates deep Snowflake platform knowledge — the formal credential
for what you already do in production.
